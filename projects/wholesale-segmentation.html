Executive summary.

On the open UCI Wholesale Customers dataset, validation selects two clusters. Test silhouette is 0.265 versus 0.157 for a median-total-spending baseline. Mean bootstrap assignment agreement on held-out customers is 0.884 adjusted Rand index.

Question and data

Can spending-based customer segments remain interpretable and stable on held-out customers?

The dataset contains 440 distributor customers and annual spending across six product categories. It is attributed to Margarida Cardoso and distributed by UCI under CC BY 4.0. Channel and Region are retained for context but excluded from clustering. No real customer identifiers are supplied.

Pipeline and evaluation

Customers split into 264 training, 88 validation, and 88 test rows. Log-one-plus transforms and standardization fit on training data only. Validation silhouette selects k from 2 through 6 for K-means. Thirty bootstrap refits of the training cohort are compared with the frozen model on the same held-out customer features using adjusted Rand agreement. Training medians describe the resulting spending profiles.

0.265test silhouette
0.884mean bootstrap ARI

Two clusters are selected. Held-out cluster counts are 36 and 52; their silhouette is 0.265. Bootstrap adjusted Rand agreement ranges from 0.662 to 1.000 at the 10th and 90th percentiles, exposing variation that one clustering score would conceal. The saved package assigns new customers using the fitted transform and centers.

Assumptions and limitations

This is a small, historical, cross-sectional dataset. Silhouette measures geometric separation rather than business usefulness. Cluster identifiers are arbitrary; high resampling agreement does not establish distinct customer types or profitable targeting. No downstream business outcomes or out-of-time stability are available.

Reproducible delivery

Importable Python modules separate configuration, data validation, domain-specific transformations, modeling or estimation, evaluation, artifact persistence, and reporting. The command-line entry point reproduces the full study with documented dependencies and seed 42.

The private repository includes mathematical and data-contract tests, a data fingerprint, configuration and library versions, machine-readable metrics, result tables, and the evaluation report. CI runs Python 3.11 and 3.12 and checks that a fresh run reproduces the saved outputs.

Code access. Source code, the full analytical design, and detailed verification logs are available on request. The project repository is private.
