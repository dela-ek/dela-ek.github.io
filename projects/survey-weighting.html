Executive summary.

Education estimates and age-domain comparisons using official person weights and all 80 SDR replicate weights. weighted bachelor-degree prevalence: 36.4%; Delaware adults age 25+: 7,554.

Question and real data

What share of Delaware residents age 25+ held at least a bachelor degree in the 2023 ACS?

  • 2023 ACS 1-year Delaware person PUMS

    Age >=25 domain, original education, sex, person weights and all 80 successive-difference replicate weights. Estimate bachelor degree or higher (SCHL>=21). No synthetic population or response process.

Pipeline and evaluation

The domain is selected using actual age. The education indicator uses SCHL>=21. Ratio estimates apply original PWGTP; uncertainty uses all 80 official successive-difference replicate weights with variance 4/80 times the sum of squared deviations from the full-weight estimate. The same estimator is recomputed within age domains. Two-sided normal 95% intervals are unbounded Wald intervals; none are near the bounds in this analysis.

Assumptions and limitations

PUMS estimates can differ from published ACS tables because of disclosure protection and microdata construction. Replicate weights represent the survey design and weight adjustments; they are not independent samples. Kish effective n summarizes weight concentration only and is not a design-adjusted effective sample size. No known population truth, observed response propensity, causal claim or synthetic nonresponse mechanism is asserted. Geography and year are fixed before analysis.

Reproducible delivery

The Python package separates verified data loading, domain transformations and estimators, analysis orchestration, result tables, scientific figures and reporting. Offline reproduction uses immutable real inputs; source acquisition and preparation scripts separately verify checksums. Current reference outputs contain actual observations and estimates.

Tests check source contracts, mathematical behavior and evaluation boundaries. The repository workflow tests Python 3.11 and 3.12, reruns the analysis and compares input fingerprints, metrics, tables and reports. Detailed source and CI logs are available on request.
