Real data behind the portfolio

All 13 current case studies use actual observations. Source code repositories remain private. Numerical findings are reproduced from the documented inputs; they are not measured commercial impact.

Scope changes: campaign response replaces unobserved retention; repeat purchase replaces unavailable KKBox churn; retail purchase recommendation replaces simulated logistics; macro nowcasting is explicitly retrospective on revised series; survey estimation uses official ACS weights. The existing wholesale segmentation already used real UCI data.

Credit-card fraud detection

Real card transactions. Chronological rare-event classification, validation-only model selection and a frozen decision threshold.

  • ULB/Worldline European card transactions, TensorFlow public tutorial mirror — All 284,807 transactions retained; no rebalancing or synthetic rows. Chronological 60/20/20 split ordered by Time; original PCA features are provider-generated. Two-day sample is not a production performance guarantee.

Retrospective macroeconomic nowcasting

Real historical macro series. Expanding-window GDP-growth estimates using revised macro indicators, with real-time availability limits stated explicitly.

  • Statsmodels US macroeconomic data, 1959Q1-2009Q3 — All 203 quarterly observations from statsmodels macrodata. Revised historical series: a retrospective contemporaneous GDP-growth model, not an as-of-vintage or deployable real-time evaluation. Current-quarter predictors assumed observed; publication lags are not modeled.

Human activity recognition

Real UCI sensor data. Six activities and 66 predeclared features; all fitting, selection and test participants remain separate.

  • UCI Human Activity Recognition Using Smartphones — All 10,299 official windows; fixed 66 time- and frequency-domain mean/std features selected by feature name, not outcomes. Official participant-separated test split retained; train validation separated by subject. Overlapping windows within subjects are never split across folds.

Retail purchase recommendations

Real retail invoices. Training-only item similarity recommends novel products in the next observed basket, compared with popularity.

  • UCI Online Retail II real purchase interactions — Positive noncancelled identified-customer invoices. Duplicate customer/invoice/item removed. Train before 2011-07-01, evaluation July-November 2011; product recommendation replaces simulated logistics interactions. All ranking candidates and similarities derived exclusively from training.

Causal inference & experimentation

Real randomized and policy studies. Randomized email conversion and paired-store employment DiD, with balance, attrition and identification limits.

  • Kevin Hillstrom email marketing randomized experiment (2008) — Original 64,000 rows; both pipelines preselect Mens E-Mail versus No E-Mail. Womens E-Mail is excluded before analysis. Two-week conversion outcome; no retention or lifetime-value labels are invented.
  • Card and Krueger New Jersey/Pennsylvania fast-food study (1992) — Original SHEET 407 is reused across two distinct state/chain records; immutable row ID preserves all 410 rows. Fixed-width parsing per original codebook; FTE=full-time + managers + 0.5 part-time. Paired complete observed interviews only; closed/nonresponding stores excluded, with counts reported.

Time to repeat purchase

Real retail invoice follow-up. Kaplan–Meier estimates, right censoring and restricted mean waiting time from actual customer invoices.

  • UCI Online Retail II real invoices (2009-2011) — Positive-value noncancelled invoices with observed customer ID, duplicate lines removed; aggregate all timestamps for the same customer/invoice, using earliest timestamp and summed value. First observed purchase is entry, next distinct invoice is event. Left-truncation/history and competing-event limitations apply; no churn labels invented.

Clinical trial analysis and missing outcomes

Real ACTG 175 trial data. Baseline-adjusted 96-week CD4 analysis, HC3 uncertainty and explicit sensitivity to missing trial outcomes.

  • ACTG 175 randomized HIV trial, CRAN speff2trial distribution — Original 2,139 participants. Analysis preselects arms 0 (ZDV) and 1 (ZDV+ddI), 96-week CD4 endpoint cd496, baseline cd40, age, and prior-antiretroviral stratum. Missing follow-up remains missing; no postrandomization predictors.
