What the tests establish

  • Fixed seeds reproduce both generated datasets.
  • DiD recovers a known effect in a noiseless panel, and adding treatment shifts the estimate by the expected amount.
  • Differential untreated trends introduce the analytically expected bias.
  • Repeating time periods does not spuriously shrink uncertainty by treating repeated market observations as independent.
  • A large randomized sample recovers the specified population effect within the test tolerance.
  • Welch uncertainty agrees with a hand calculation.
  • The event-study reference is zero, and the deliberate trend violation is detected.
  • Duplicate, missing, and inconsistent records are rejected.

What reproduction checks

The workflow regenerates all data, effect estimates, the event-study table, figures, and the results report. It compares data fingerprints exactly, numerical estimates within floating-point tolerances, and the report text exactly. SVG figures are regenerated; visual rendering is not compared pixel by pixel across systems.

The observed outcomes, assumptions, and interval limitations remain part of the case study and design notes. Passing software checks does not establish that these assumptions hold for a real intervention.
